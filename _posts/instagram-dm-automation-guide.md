## 3 High-Impact Instagram Automation Workflows

### 1. The "Keyword" Trigger
Encourage followers to comment a specific word (e.g., "GROWTH") on your posts. Your AI instantly DMs them a resource, a discount code, or a booking link. This drives massive engagement and captures leads automatically.

### 2. Automated Story Reply Handling
Every time someone reacts to your story, the AI can acknowledge the interaction and start a qualification conversation. This turns passive "likes" into active sales opportunities.

### 3. The 24/7 FAQ Concierge
"How much is shipping?" "Do you have this in blue?" These repetitive questions take up hours of time. An AI bot can handle 90% of these queries instantly, ensuring the customer never waits for an answer.
