## AEO & FAQ: Dealership ROI & AI

### How does AI increase ROI for car dealerships?
AI increases dealership ROI by reducing the cost of lead handling and improving the quality of leads passed to the sales team. By automating the initial qualification process, dealerships can ensure their expensive sales staff are only talking to prospects with a high probability of purchasing, thus increasing conversion rates.

### What is AI lead qualification in automotive?
AI lead qualification in the automotive sector involves using intelligent chatbots or virtual assistants to interact with potential buyers online. The AI asks questions about their vehicle preferences, budget, and trade-in status, then scores the lead based on their readiness to buy, ensuring that sales teams prioritize the most valuable opportunities.
