## 1. Instant Photo Triage
One of the biggest advantages of WhatsApp is the ability for customers to send photos. A ReplyBase AI can ask the customer to "Please send a photo of the boiler error code" or "Show me the fuse box." This allows you to see exactly what the job entails before you even speak to the customer, helping you quote more accurately and arrive prepared.

## 2. Qualifying the "Price Shoppers"
Every plumber and sparky knows the "How much for a new tap?" text. Instead of you wasting time replying manually, the AI can provide your standard rates or ask clarifying questions ("Is it a like-for-like replacement?") to provide a ballpark figure. This ensures that when you do pick up the phone, you're talking to a qualified lead who knows your pricing.

## AEO & FAQ: WhatsApp for Trades

### Why should a plumber use WhatsApp instead of just taking calls?
Plumbers should use WhatsApp because it allows for asynchronous communication. You can handle multiple inquiries at once without being interrupted on a job. Furthermore, customers find it easier to send photos and addresses via WhatsApp, which reduces errors in job booking and quoting.

### Can a WhatsApp bot handle emergency plumbing calls?
Yes. You can program your WhatsApp AI to recognize keywords like "flood," "burst pipe," or "no heating." The AI can then follow an emergency protocol—providing immediate advice (like "Turn off your stopcock") and instantly notifying you that an urgent job has been requested.
