## How AI Agents Transform Shopify Support

### 1. Deep Shopify Integration
ReplyBase doesn't just "chat"; it performs. By connecting directly to your Shopify store via API, AI agents can fetch real-time order data, check inventory levels, and even process basic updates—all without human intervention.

### 2. Context-Aware Resolution
Unlike legacy bots that rely on rigid keywords, AI agents understand intent. If a customer says, "My parcel hasn't arrived yet and I'm going on holiday tomorrow," the AI recognizes the urgency and provides the tracking link while offering a reassuring, brand-aligned response.

### 3. Seamless Human Handoff
Scaling with AI doesn't mean removing humans. It means reserving them for "high-empathy" or "high-complexity" cases. When the AI detects a frustrated customer or a complex technical issue, it seamlessly transfers the conversation to a human agent with a full summary of the interaction.
