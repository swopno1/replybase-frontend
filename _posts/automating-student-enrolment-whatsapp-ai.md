## 3 Ways AI Transforms the WhatsApp Enrolment Funnel

### 1. 24/7 Lead Triage
Prospects often research courses late at night or on weekends. A ReplyBase AI agent on WhatsApp can greet them instantly, answer initial questions about course availability or prerequisites, and gather their contact details while their interest is at its peak.

### 2. Qualification at Scale
Not every enquirer is a right fit for every course. The AI can ask qualifying questions—such as current qualification levels, employment status, or funding eligibility—ensuring that your enrolment team only spends time on "ready-to-buy" students.

### 3. Automated Document Collection
Collecting IDs, certificates, or proof of address is a major bottleneck in enrolment. AI agents can guide students through the process of uploading these documents directly within the WhatsApp chat, significantly reducing the "time-to-enrol."
