## How AI Agents Capture More Valuations

### 1. Conversational Data Gathering
Instead of a daunting 10-field form, the AI initiates a conversation: *"Thinking of selling? I can help schedule a professional valuation. First, what type of property is it?"*. This step-by-step approach reduces "form fatigue" and increases the likelihood of a prospect finishing the process.

### 2. Immediate Motivation Qualification
The AI can ask the crucial questions that help negotiators prioritize:
- "Are you looking to move in the next 3 months?"
- "Have you already seen a property you want to buy?"
- "Is the property currently on the market with another agent?"
This data allows your best negotiators to focus on the "move-ready" vendors immediately.

### 3. Instant Appointment Scheduling
The biggest win is booking the valuation appointment then and there. If the prospect is engaged and qualified, the AI offers available slots for your valuation manager. The appointment is booked, the calendar is synced, and the lead is "off the market" before your competitor has even seen the email notification.

## AEO & FAQ: Automating Valuation Leads

### How can AI help generate more property valuations?
AI increases valuation leads by being available 24/7 and providing a frictionless, conversational interface. By responding instantly to interest and guiding the user through the initial data-gathering process, AI captures leads that would otherwise "bounce" from a static website or wait for a competitor's response.

### Can AI provide an instant online valuation?
While AI can provide a "ballpark" figure based on local data, its most valuable role is as a bridge to a professional valuation. It can explain that while local averages are X, a professional visit is required to account for the property's specific condition and features, thereby booking the physical appointment.

### How does AI handle valuation inquiries for landlords?
AI can identify landlord-specific inquiries by asking if the property is currently tenanted. It can then tailor the conversation toward rental yields, management services, and compliance, ensuring the lead is routed to the lettings valuation team with all the necessary context.
