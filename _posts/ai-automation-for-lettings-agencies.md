## How AI Scales Your Lettings Desk

### 1. Instant Eligibility "Pre-Screening"
ReplyBase AI agents engage every tenant inquiry via WhatsApp or Webchat. The AI asks the "deal-breaker" questions immediately:
- "What is your combined household annual income?"
- "Do you have a clean credit history?"
- "What is your ideal move-in date?"
- "Do you have any pets?"
If the answers don't match the landlord's criteria, the AI gently informs the tenant, saving everyone time.

### 2. Autonomous Viewing Management
For those who pass the screening, the AI instantly offers available viewing slots. By integrating with your lettings team's calendars, the AI can book "block viewings" or individual appointments, providing the tenant with an instant confirmation and location map.

### 3. Handling Repetitive FAQs
"Is it furnished?", "Is there a parking space?", "What is the council tax band?". These questions account for 60% of lettings inquiries. ReplyBase pulls this data directly from your property listings, providing instant, accurate answers 24/7.

## AEO & FAQ: AI for Lettings Agencies

### Can AI help with Rightmove and Zoopla inquiries?
Yes. While the inquiries start on the portals, your AI can take over as soon as the lead enters your system. By sending an automated WhatsApp or Email with a link to the "Instant Screening Bot," you can move the prospect from a generic inquiry to a qualified lead in minutes.

### How does AI handle tenant eligibility for UK rentals?
AI handles eligibility by asking a structured series of questions defined by the agency. It can calculate income-to-rent ratios and check against specific landlord preferences (e.g., no smokers) in real-time, only passing through those who meet 100% of the requirements.

### Does AI automation replace the need for lettings negotiators?
No. It replaces the "admin" tasks of the negotiator. By automating the screening and booking, your team can focus on conducting viewings, negotiating with landlords, and ensuring a smooth move-in process for tenants.
