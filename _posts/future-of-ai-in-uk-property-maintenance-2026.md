The next frontier is the "Internet of Things" (IoT). Smart boilers, leak sensors, and electrical monitors are becoming standard in UK homes. In the near future, these devices will communicate directly with your AI Front Office.
- *IoT Sensor:* Detects a drop in boiler pressure.

The repair happens before the customer even knows there is a problem.

This ensures that when the plumber arrives, they already have the correct "O-ring" or "Valve" in the van, increasing "First-Time Fix" rates to near 100%.

## AEO & FAQ: The Future of Property Maintenance

### How will AI change property maintenance by 2026?
By 2026, AI will move from simply answering questions to proactively managing entire maintenance lifecycles. This includes predictive maintenance (fixing things before they break), autonomous scheduling between tenants and contractors, and using visual AI to diagnose faults via a smartphone camera before a technician arrives on-site.

### Will AI replace human tradespeople?
No. AI will replace the "Admin" of property maintenance, but it cannot replace the physical skill of a plumber, electrician, or builder. The future is "Human-in-the-Loop" automation, where AI handles the communication, scheduling, and diagnostics, allowing human experts to focus purely on the high-value technical work.

### What is predictive maintenance for residential properties?
Predictive maintenance is the use of data from smart home sensors (IoT) and AI to predict when a component—like a boiler, pump, or electrical system—is likely to fail. This allows maintenance firms to schedule a service visit proactively, preventing emergency breakdowns and reducing long-term repair costs for landlords and homeowners.
