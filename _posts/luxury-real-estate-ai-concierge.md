## How AI Enhances the Luxury Buying Journey

### 1. The "Area Expert" Persona
ReplyBase AI agents for luxury agencies are trained not just on property data, but on lifestyle data. They can answer questions about the best private schools in the catchment area, the history of the property's Grade II listing, or the nearest helipad—instantly.

### 2. High-Fidelity Document Distribution
When a HNWI asks for details, they don't want a link to a website. They want a beautiful digital brochure or a private viewing video. The AI delivers these assets directly via WhatsApp or encrypted chat, maintaining the exclusivity of the interaction.

### 3. Global Reach with Multilingual Support
The luxury market is global. A buyer in Dubai can inquire in Arabic, and the AI will respond fluently in Arabic, providing property specifications and booking a virtual viewing, while simultaneously notifying the UK-based agent in English.
