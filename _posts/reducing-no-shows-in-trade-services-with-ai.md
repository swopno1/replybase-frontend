## 1. The "Confirmation Loop"
Trust starts with confirmation. As soon as a job is booked via your AI agent or phone, the system should send a professional WhatsApp message. This moves the appointment from a "casual conversation" to a "firm commitment" in the customer's mind. Including a "Save to Calendar" link further ensures the job is visible on their device.

## 2. Multi-Channel 24-Hour Reminders
A simple SMS can be missed. A multi-channel approach—WhatsApp followed by a fallback SMS—is far more effective. The AI sends a polite message: *"Hi [Name], just a reminder that Dave is scheduled to visit tomorrow between 10 AM and 12 PM. Could you please reply 'YES' to confirm or 'RE' if you need to move it?"*

If the customer doesn't reply, the AI can escalate a notification to you, allowing you to fill that slot before you even leave the house.

## 3. "Engineer on the Way" Transparency
Anxiety is often the cause of no-shows. If a customer isn't sure you're coming, they might nip out to the shops. An automated "I'm 20 minutes away" message, triggered by your AI when you start the journey, provides the "Uber-style" experience that modern customers expect. It ensures they are home, have cleared the workspace, and are ready for your arrival.
