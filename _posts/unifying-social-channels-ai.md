As a business grows, so does its "digital footprint." You start with a website, then an Instagram, then a Facebook Page, and then a WhatsApp Business account. Suddenly, your team is juggling four different apps just to answer one customer's questions.

This fragmentation is more than just an annoyance—it’s an operational bottleneck that stifles growth.

## The Cost of Fragmented Communication

When your communication channels are siloed:
1. **Context is Lost:** A customer who asks a question on Facebook and follows up on WhatsApp is treated as two different people.
2. **Response Times Suffer:** Your team has to constantly switch tabs and check notifications, leading to delays.
3. **Data is Wasted:** You have no central record of what your customers are actually asking for across the board.
