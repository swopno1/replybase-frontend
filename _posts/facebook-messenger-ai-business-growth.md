Messenger is a "low-friction" channel. Users already have the app, they know how to use it, and they trust the interface. For businesses, this means lower barriers to entry for starting a sales conversation compared to a traditional website form.

## Scaling Your Facebook Presence with AI

### 1. Instant Lead Qualification
When a prospect clicks a "Send Message" button on your Facebook Ad, the AI takes over. It can ask for their email, phone number, and specific needs, ensuring your sales team only spends time on "hot" leads.

### 2. Seamless Human Hand-off
AI handles the bulk of the work, but some questions require a human. ReplyBase allows for a seamless transition where the AI notifies a team member to jump into the Messenger chat when a complex or high-value situation arises.

### 3. Re-engagement Campaigns
Unlike a website visitor who leaves and is gone, a Messenger user is a "subscriber." You can use the platform to send automated follow-ups or updates (within Meta’s guidelines) to bring them back into the sales funnel.
