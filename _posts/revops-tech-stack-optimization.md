## 1. Consolidating the Communication Layer
Most businesses use separate tools for webchat, WhatsApp, social DMs, and SMS. AI-driven RevOps unifies these into a single "Conversational Layer." By using one platform to manage all channels, you eliminate data leakage and ensure a consistent brand experience for every lead.

## 2. Native AI Lead Processing
Instead of using a chatbot builder, a lead qualification tool, and a meeting scheduler, look for a platform that does all three natively. This reduces latency and ensures that data flows perfectly from the first "Hello" to the booked meeting in your CRM. Consolidation here is the key to conversion speed.

## 3. Simplifying the Data Flow
An optimized RevOps stack has a simple data architecture: All interactions happen in the AI hub, and all data is pushed to the CRM. This "Hub and Spoke" model is much easier to maintain and troubleshoot than a "Web" model where every tool tries to talk to every other tool.
