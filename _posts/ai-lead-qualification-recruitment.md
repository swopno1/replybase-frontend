AI improves candidate lead qualification by providing instant, standardized screening 24/7. It can ask essential questions, verify credentials, and assess fit against job requirements immediately, ensuring that recruiters only spend time speaking with highly qualified and interested talent.

### Can I use an AI chatbot on WhatsApp for recruitment?
Yes, you can use an AI chatbot on WhatsApp for recruitment by integrating ReplyBase with the WhatsApp Business API. This allows you to automate candidate enquiries, conduct initial screenings, and even schedule interviews directly through the UK's most popular messaging app.
