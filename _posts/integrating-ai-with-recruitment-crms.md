For many UK recruitment agencies, the CRM (Customer Relationship Management) or ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is a "data graveyard"—a place where candidate information goes to be forgotten because it's too difficult to keep updated.

In 2026, the leading agencies are "closing the loop" by integrating their AI communication channels directly with their CRM. This turns the CRM from a static database into a live, self-updating engine of growth.

## The Problem with Manual Data Entry

Recruiters are salespeople, not data entry clerks. When a consultant has to manually type notes from a WhatsApp call into Bullhorn or Vincere, one of two things happens:
1. They do it poorly, leading to a "dirty" database.
2. They don't do it at all, leading to lost institutional knowledge.

## How to Integrate Your Recruitment CRM with AI

ReplyBase is designed to sit at the center of your recruitment tech stack, connecting your comms to your core database.

### 1. Automated Candidate Capture
When a new candidate engages with your AI via WhatsApp or Webchat, ReplyBase can automatically check your CRM. If they don't exist, a new record is created with their name, number, and source. If they do exist, the new interaction is simply added to their existing timeline.

### 2. Conversational Field Mapping
During an automated screening, every answer provided by the candidate can be mapped to a specific field in your CRM. Notice period, salary expectations, and primary skill set are updated in real-time, ensuring your database is always "search-ready."

### 3. Trigger-Based Workflows
The integration allows for powerful automation. If a candidate passes an AI screen, ReplyBase can trigger a notification in the CRM for the relevant consultant, or even move the candidate to a specific stage in the hiring pipeline (e.g., "Ready for Interview").
