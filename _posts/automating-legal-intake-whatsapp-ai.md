## Why WhatsApp for Legal Services?

Email inboxes are cluttered, and phone lines are often busy. WhatsApp offers a 98% open rate and provides an instant, personal connection that traditional channels lack. For a client facing a legal issue, the ability to send a message and receive an instant, professional response from an AI agent is a major relief and a strong trust builder.

## Key Benefits of WhatsApp AI Intake

### 1. 24/7 Inquiry Handling
Legal emergencies don't wait for office hours. Whether it's an employment dispute, a property matter, or a criminal defence inquiry, a ReplyBase WhatsApp agent is always available to gather initial case details, provide basic procedural information, and manage client expectations instantly.

### 2. Conversational Evidence Gathering
Instead of sending a complex PDF form, your AI agent can lead the client through a conversation. It can ask for specific details about their matter, and the client can even upload photos or documents directly within the WhatsApp chat. This information is then packaged and delivered to your legal team, ready for review.

### 3. Immediate Fee-Earner Handoff
Once the AI has qualified the lead and gathered the necessary information, it can instantly notify the relevant department or fee-earner via your CRM or Slack. This ensures that high-value cases are prioritised and the human solicitor can step in with all the context they need to win the instruction.
