## The Shift in Student Engagement

Modern students—from Gen Z undergraduates to career-changing professionals—expect immediate answers. AI allows institutions to capture this high-intent interest instantly, providing the "Gold Standard" of service that modern learners demand.

## Strategic Pillars of EdTech Excellence

### 1. Frictionless Student Enrolment
The "leaky bucket" in education is the gap between enquiry and enrolment. ReplyBase AI agents engage prospects in a natural, conversational flow via WhatsApp or Webchat, gathering essential details (previous qualifications, course interest, funding status) and qualifying them for the next stage of the funnel instantly.

### 2. 24/7 Multi-Channel Support
Students don't just have questions during office hours. Whether it's a query about campus facilities on Messenger or a question about course modules on WhatsApp, AI provides consistent, accurate answers 24/7. This reduces the burden on administrative staff while ensuring students feel supported at every touchpoint.

### 3. Automated Open Day Coordination
Managing open day bookings and attendance is a logistical headache. AI agents handle the entire process: from initial enquiry and booking to sending automated WhatsApp reminders and post-event follow-ups, ensuring maximum attendance and recruitment ROI.
