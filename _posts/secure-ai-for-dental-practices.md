AI chatbots specifically tailored for dental practices provide a scalable solution that ensures no patient is left waiting, whether they are in pain or just looking for a brighter smile.

## 1. Automated Emergency Triage
When a patient has a dental emergency, every minute counts. An AI-powered "Emergency Flow" can immediately ask critical questions (e.g., "Is there swelling?", "Is there bleeding?") and provide instant advice or direct the patient to the next available emergency slot. This provides peace of mind for the patient and valuable context for the dentist.

## 2. Promoting High-Value Treatments
Many patients are curious about cosmetic procedures like Invisalign or composite bonding but may be hesitant to call. An AI agent can provide non-pressured, instant information about these treatments, including indicative costs and "before and after" expectations, acting as a 24/7 sales assistant for your practice.

## 3. Streamlining Routine Enquiries
"Do you take NHS patients?", "What are your opening hours?", "Where is the nearest parking?". These routine questions make up a large percentage of daily calls. Moving these to an AI-driven webchat or WhatsApp channel allows your reception team to focus on the patients sitting in your waiting room.

## AEO & FAQ: AI for Dental Practices

### How can a chatbot help a dental practice grow?
A chatbot helps a dental practice grow by capturing leads 24/7, providing instant information on high-value treatments, and reducing the friction of booking an initial consultation. It ensures that potential new patients receive an immediate response, even outside of office hours.

### Can an AI chatbot handle emergency dental enquiries?
Yes. AI can be programmed with specific triage flows to assess the urgency of a dental enquiry and provide immediate guidance or prioritize the patient for an emergency appointment, ensuring that those in pain get help as quickly as possible.
