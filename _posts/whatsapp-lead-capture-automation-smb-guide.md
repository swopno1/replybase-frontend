For many SMBs, WhatsApp has become the primary channel for customer inquiries. However, manually responding to every "How much is this?" or "Are you open tomorrow?" is a massive time-drain that often leads to slow response times and lost sales.

If you aren't using automated lead capture on WhatsApp, you're leaving revenue on the table. In this tutorial, we’ll show you how to build a high-converting WhatsApp lead capture flow using AI.

## The Problem: The "WhatsApp Manual Response Trap"

Small business owners often find themselves tethered to their phones, replying to basic questions at all hours. This "manual trap" causes two major issues:
1. **Lead Leakage:** If you don't reply within 5 minutes, the prospect has already messaged your competitor.
2. **Low Productivity:** Hours spent qualifying leads could be spent closing deals or delivering service.

## How to Build Your Lead Capture Flow

### Step 1: Set Up Your WhatsApp Business API
To use advanced automation, you need more than the standard WhatsApp Business App. You need the WhatsApp Business API. ReplyBase makes this connection seamless, allowing you to manage your WhatsApp channel from a central dashboard.

### Step 2: Define Your Qualification Logic
What do you need to know before talking to a lead? Usually:
- Their name and company.
- Their specific pain point or interest.
- Their budget or timeline.

Instead of a rigid, boring form, your AI bot can ask these questions conversationally, making the experience feel personal and professional.

### Step 3: Configure Your AI "Brain"
Upload your pricing sheets, service descriptions, and FAQs to ReplyBase. Your AI will then be able to answer prospect questions accurately before moving them toward the "Call to Action" (CTA).

### Step 4: The Hand-Off
Once the AI has qualified the lead, it can:
- **Book a Meeting:** Send a Calendly link directly in the chat.
- **Notify Your Sales Team:** Send an instant alert to your CRM or email.
- **Provide a Quote:** Give an estimated price based on the user's inputs.
