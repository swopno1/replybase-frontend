The primary distinction between the two platforms lies in their architectural DNA.

## Why Startups are Making the Switch

### 1. Cost Transparency and Predictability
Intercom's pricing can be notoriously complex for scaling startups. Between seat costs and "per-resolution" fees for their AI bot, monthly bills can become unpredictable. ReplyBase offers simple, usage-based tiers that allow founders to budget with confidence.

### 2. Focus on WhatsApp and Omnichannel
While Intercom supports multiple channels, ReplyBase is specifically optimized for the UK market's reliance on **WhatsApp Business**. Our platform makes it significantly easier to build complex, automated WhatsApp flows that qualify leads and book meetings without human input.

### 3. No-Code Operational Agility
Startups need to move fast. ReplyBase’s visual builder allows a non-technical founder or ops lead to deploy a fully functional AI support system in minutes, rather than requiring a dedicated "Intercom Admin" to manage complex workflows.
