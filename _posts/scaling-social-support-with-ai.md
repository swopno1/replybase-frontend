The "Social Support Gap" is the time between a customer's inquiry on social media and a business's response. Research shows that 40% of consumers expect a response on social media within 60 minutes. If you miss that window, your brand perception drops instantly.

## How AI Scales Your Support Operations

### 1. Instant Deflection of 80% of Queries
Most social support inquiries are repetitive: "Where is my order?" "What are your hours?" "Do you ship to the UK?" AI handles these instantly, freeing your human team to focus on the 20% of complex cases that actually need their empathy.

### 2. Multi-Lingual Capability
If you sell globally, you need support in multiple languages. Modern AI models, like those powering ReplyBase, can understand and respond in dozens of languages fluently, allowing you to scale into new markets without hiring local support teams.

### 3. Intelligent Triage and Escalation
Not all messages are created equal. An AI agent can identify a "frustrated" customer or a "high-value" lead through sentiment analysis and escalate them to a human team member immediately via Slack or Email.
