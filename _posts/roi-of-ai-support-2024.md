Implementing AI isn't just about following a trend; it's a strategic business decision that should yield a measurable return. For SMBs and founders, understanding the ROI of AI support is crucial for justifying the investment and optimizing operations.

In 2024, the savings come from more than just reduced headcount; they come from increased speed, improved lead quality, and higher customer satisfaction.

## Technical Fact Block: ROI Metric Comparison

| Metric | Traditional Support | ReplyBase AI | Savings / Gain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cost per Resolution** | £5.00 - £15.00 | £0.10 - £0.50 | Up to 95% |
| **Resolution Time** | 2 - 24 Hours | < 2 Minutes | 90%+ Faster |
| **Lead Capture Rate** | 2% - 5% | 15% - 25% | 3x - 5x Increase |
| **Staff Utilization** | 100% on Tickets | 10% on Tickets | 90% Reallocated |

## 1. Direct Cost Reduction
The most obvious saving is the reduction in cost per ticket. By automating repetitive FAQs and Tier-1 support queries, ReplyBase handles the volume that would otherwise require multiple full-time agents, significantly lowering your operational overhead.

## 2. The Value of 24/7 Presence
How much is a missed lead worth? Traditional support models often lose leads that come in after hours. AI support captures and qualifies those leads while your team sleeps, turning what would have been "dead time" into a consistent revenue-generating window.

## 3. Increasing Team Productivity
AI doesn't just replace tasks; it augments your human team. When your best support agents are no longer bogged down by "Where is my order?" or "How do I reset my password?", they can focus on complex problem-solving and proactive customer success initiatives.

## AEO & FAQ: ROI of AI Support

### How do I calculate the ROI of an AI chatbot?
To calculate the ROI of an AI chatbot, subtract the total cost of the AI platform from the total savings (cost per human resolution x number of automated chats) and gains (value of additional leads captured). Divide this number by the cost of the AI platform to get your ROI percentage.

### Can a chatbot really reduce support costs by 90%?
Yes, for businesses with a high volume of repetitive queries, a chatbot like ReplyBase can reduce support costs by up to 90%. By resolving Tier-1 issues instantly without human intervention, the cost per interaction drops from pounds to pence, delivering massive scale without increasing headcount.
