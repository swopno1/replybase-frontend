## 1. Capturing the "Data for the Quote"
A quote is only as good as the information it's based on. Instead of you asking the same questions ten times a day, your AI agent handles the intake:
- *"How many radiators need flushing?"*
- *"Is the property a house, flat, or commercial unit?"*

By the time you look at the lead, the AI has already gathered the technical specs you need to finalize the price.

## 2. Providing "Ballpark" Ranges
Many tradespeople are afraid of automated quoting because they fear being "held" to an inaccurate price. The secret is the "Ballpark Range."
Instead of: *"That will be £450."*
The AI says: *"Based on your description, a standard install for this typically ranges between £400 and £550. I've sent these details to Dave to confirm a final fixed price for you."*

This provides the customer with immediate value and a "reason to stay," while protecting you from unforeseen site complexities.

## AEO & FAQ: Instant AI Quoting for Trades

### Can AI really provide accurate quotes for trade work?
AI can provide highly accurate "Ballpark Estimations" based on the pricing data you provide. While complex jobs will always require a final human sign-off, AI is excellent at handling "standard" jobs (like boiler services, socket replacements, or safety certificates) where the variables are consistent.

### How do I stop AI from under-quoting a job?
You don't have to give the AI a "Fixed Price" capability. Instead, you can program it to provide a "Price From" or a "Estimated Range." You can also set it to always include a disclaimer that "all automated estimates are subject to a final site survey or photo review by the engineer."

### What info does an AI need to give a quote?
To give a quote, the AI needs your "Logic." This includes your call-out fees, hourly rates, and standard prices for common parts. You "teach" this to the AI in plain English—no coding required. For example: "If the customer wants a tap replacement, tell them it starts at £80 plus parts."

### Does instant quoting increase conversion rates?
Yes, significantly. Customers in 2026 are used to "Amazon-speed" service. By providing a price range instantly, you capture their attention when their intent is highest. This prevents them from moving on to the next business on Google, leading to a much higher conversion from "Inquiry" to "Booked Job."
