## Strategic Pillars of Modern PropTech

### 1. Instant Property Data Distribution
Potential buyers often have specific questions: "Is the property leasehold or freehold?", "What is the service charge?", or "Is there parking?". Instead of waiting for a negotiator to check the files, ReplyBase agents pull this data from your brochures instantly, providing accurate answers in seconds.

### 2. Conversational Viewing Scheduling
The friction of booking a viewing is a major conversion killer. AI agents allow prospects to book directly into your team's calendar via WhatsApp. The AI checks availability, confirms the slot, and sends a calendar invite, all without a single human intervention.

### 3. Automated Landlord & Vendor Capture
AI isn't just for buyers. Strategic agencies use AI agents to handle valuation requests. By asking qualifying questions about property type, location, and timeline, the AI identifies high-intent vendors and landlords, handing them off to your senior negotiators for the close.
