## 1. Meeting the Buyer Where They Are
With over 40 million users in the UK, WhatsApp is the dominant communication tool. Allowing a buyer to book a test drive within their favourite app removes the barrier of having to answer a call from an unknown number. ReplyBase enables this seamless transition from browsing to booking.

One of the biggest frustrations for buyers is booking a test drive only to find the vehicle is unavailable. ReplyBase integrates with your dealership management system (DMS) and staff calendars to ensure that bookings are only made when both the vehicle and a product specialist are available.

## 3. Automated Qualification & Pre-Visit Prep
An AI agent doesn't just book the time; it can also ask crucial qualification questions. *Do you have a part-exchange? Are you looking for finance? What is your current vehicle?* This data is synced to your CRM, allowing your sales team to be fully prepared for the customer's arrival, creating a more personalized and professional experience.
