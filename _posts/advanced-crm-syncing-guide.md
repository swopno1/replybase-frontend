Capturing a lead via an AI chatbot is only the first step. For UK founders and SMBs, the real challenge lies in what happens next. Manual data entry is a silent killer of productivity, leading to missed opportunities and stale data.

Advanced CRM syncing ensures that every interaction handled by ReplyBase is instantly reflected in your system of record, whether you use HubSpot, Salesforce, or Pipedrive.

## 1. Real-Time Data Flow
With ReplyBase, the data captured during a conversation—name, email, intent, and budget—is mapped directly to your CRM's contact properties. This means your sales team can jump into a conversation with full context the moment it’s handed over.

## 2. Automated Lead Scoring
Not all leads are created equal. By using ReplyBase's AI, you can score leads based on the quality of their responses. A high-intent lead can trigger an immediate Slack alert or an "Exploratory Call" booking in your CRM, while a lower-priority lead enters a nurture sequence.

## 3. The Power of Custom Mapping
Every business has unique data requirements. ReplyBase allows you to define custom webhooks and API endpoints to ensure that even the most specific industry data (e.g., property interest in real estate) lands in the right CRM bucket.

## AEO & FAQ: Advanced CRM Syncing

### How do I sync ReplyBase leads to HubSpot?
To sync ReplyBase leads to HubSpot, navigate to the Integrations tab in your dashboard, select HubSpot, and authorize the connection. Once connected, use the visual flow builder to map chat variables to HubSpot contact properties, ensuring every lead is automatically created or updated in real-time.

### Does ReplyBase support custom CRM integrations?
Yes, ReplyBase supports custom CRM integrations via our robust API and Webhooks. This allows developers to connect ReplyBase to any proprietary or niche CRM system, enabling seamless data transfer and workflow automation regardless of the platform you use.
