For years, social media ROI has been a "fuzzy" metric—likes, shares, and impressions. But in the era of AI automation, we can finally move beyond vanity metrics and look at the real impact on the bottom line.

If you are using AI to manage your social channels, you aren't just "engaging"; you are "operating." Here is how to measure the value of that operation.

## The 3 Pillars of AI ROI

When calculating the return on investment for social media AI, we look at three distinct areas:
1. **Operational Savings:** The cost of labor saved by automating support and lead gen.
2. **Revenue Expansion:** The value of leads captured and sales closed that would have otherwise been lost.
3. **Strategic Advantage:** The long-term value of data continuity and 24/7 brand presence.

## Technical Fact Block: Calculating the "ReplyBase Value"

| Metric | Calculation Method | Typical Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Labor Savings** | (Hours saved per month) x (Hourly rate) | £2,000 - £5,000+ per month |
| **Lead Recovery** | (Missed DMs) x (Avg. Lead Value) | 15-25% Increase in Pipeline |

| **Retention Value** | (Reduction in churn) via instant support | High (LTV impact) |

## Moving Beyond "Time Saved"

While "time saved" is the easiest metric to track, the real power of AI lies in **Lead Quality**. By using AI to qualify every DM and Messenger inquiry, your sales team is only talking to the top 10% of prospects. This drastically improves the efficiency of your most expensive resource: your human sales closers.
