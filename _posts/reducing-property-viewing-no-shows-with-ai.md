For a busy UK estate agent, there is nothing more frustrating—or costly—than traveling to a property only for the prospect to "no-show." Each missed viewing represents wasted travel time, lost fuel costs, and, most importantly, a missed opportunity for the vendor or landlord.

In 2026, the industry has solved the "ghosting" problem using **AI-powered WhatsApp reminders**, which achieve significantly higher engagement than legacy email or SMS systems.

## The Cost of the "No-Show"

The impact of a no-show goes beyond just the 30 minutes at the property. It disrupts the negotiator's entire schedule and can damage the relationship with the property owner.

- **Economic Cost:** Average cost of £45 per missed viewing (time + fuel).
- **Opportunity Cost:** Negotiators could be closing deals elsewhere.
- **Brand Cost:** Vendors lose faith in the agency's ability to manage their property.

## How AI Reminders Eliminate Ghosting

### 1. Interactive Confirmation (24h Before)
ReplyBase AI doesn't just send a one-way notification. It sends an interactive WhatsApp message 24 hours before the viewing: *"Hi [Name], just confirming our viewing at [Property Address] tomorrow at [Time]. Are you still able to make it?"*. The user can reply "Yes" or "Need to Reschedule" with a single tap.

### 2. The "Last-Mile" Prompt (2h Before)
A final, short prompt is sent two hours before the appointment. If the prospect's plans have changed at the last minute, the AI identifies this immediately and notifies the negotiator before they even leave the office.

### 3. Automated Re-booking for Missed Appointments
If a prospect does indicate they can't make it, the AI doesn't just cancel. It immediately offers alternative slots from the negotiator's calendar, turning a potential loss into a rescheduled opportunity instantly.
