## Why Email is Failing Modern SMBs

Email was designed for asynchronous, long-form communication. It is formal, easily ignored, and often buried under mountains of promotional noise. For a modern consumer who expects instant gratification, email feels slow and disconnected.

WhatsApp, on the other hand, is where your customers already spend their time. It is personal, immediate, and carries a level of trust that email cannot replicate.

## The "Conversational Premium"

When you communicate via WhatsApp, you aren't just sending a message; you are starting a dialogue. This "conversational premium" leads to:
1. **Lower Friction:** Customers can ask quick questions without the overhead of "Dear Team..."
2. **Higher Trust:** Conversational threads feel more human and less like a broadcast.
3. **Better Data:** AI-powered tools like ReplyBase can analyze these conversations to provide deep insights into customer pain points.
