## 1. Capturing the "I Need It Now" Customer
In the trade services industry, the first business to reply usually wins the job. If a customer has a burst pipe or a power cut, they aren't going to wait two hours for a callback. An AI agent on your website or WhatsApp can engage them instantly, gather their details, and confirm that "someone is on the way" or "we've received your request and qualified it as urgent."

## 2. Automating the Triage Process
Not every call is an emergency. AI agents can be trained to ask the right questions:
- "Is there water leaking through the ceiling?"
- "Do you have any power at all?"
- "Is this for a domestic or commercial property?"

By triaging these inquiries, the AI ensures that you only get interrupted for high-value, urgent work, while routine quotes are scheduled for the next business day.
