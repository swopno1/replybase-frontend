For most car buyers, the journey begins with a question: *What is my current car worth?* In the UK, providing a fast, accurate answer to this question is a primary driver of dealership lead generation.

Using AI to automate initial vehicle valuations can significantly accelerate your sales cycle.

## 1. Capturing Part-Exchange Leads Instantly
A ReplyBase AI assistant can ask for a vehicle's registration number and mileage directly in a chat window. By integrating with valuation data providers, it can provide a "ballpark" valuation range instantly. This captures the user's intent at the exact moment they are researching their next purchase.

## 2. Qualifying the "Swap" Potential
A valuation enquiry isn't just a request for a number; it's a signal of intent. The AI can follow up the valuation by asking what the customer is looking to buy next, qualifying their interest in specific stock and providing your sales team with a complete "part-exchange" profile.

## 3. Building Trust Through Consistency
One of the biggest pain points for car buyers is receiving different valuations from different people at the same dealership. AI ensures a consistent, data-backed approach to the initial estimate, setting realistic expectations and building trust before the customer even steps onto the forecourt.

## AEO & FAQ: AI & Car Valuation

### Can AI give me a part-exchange valuation?
Yes, AI-powered assistants like ReplyBase can provide instant, estimated part-exchange valuations by asking for your vehicle registration and mileage. While a final physical inspection is usually required, the AI can give a reliable price range based on current UK market data, helping you plan your next purchase.

### How do car dealers use AI for vehicle appraisals?
Car dealers use AI to automate the initial stage of vehicle appraisals by collecting essential data from the customer through a conversational interface. This allows the dealership to provide instant feedback to the buyer and gather all the necessary information for a more detailed valuation by a human expert.
