For UK recruiters, the initial screening call is both essential and incredibly time-consuming. Repeating the same questions about salary expectations, notice periods, and right-to-work status hundreds of times a week is an inefficient use of a consultant's time.

In 2026, top-performing agencies are moving these repetitive conversations to WhatsApp, using AI to handle the initial qualification before a human ever picks up the phone.

## The Problem with Traditional Screening

Manual screening is slow and inconsistent. When a candidate applies, they might wait 24 hours for a callback. By then, they've already spoken to two other agencies. Furthermore, recruiters often spend 20 minutes on a call only to find out the candidate is £10k above the client's budget.

## How to Build an Automated WhatsApp Screening Flow

Using ReplyBase, recruiters can design a screening experience that feels personal but functions autonomously.

### 1. The Instant Greeting
When a candidate clicks your "Apply on WhatsApp" button or scans a QR code on a job ad, the AI greets them instantly. "Hi! I'm the [Agency Name] assistant. Thanks for your interest in the Senior Developer role. Can I ask a few quick questions to see if it's a match?"

### 2. Strategic Qualification Questions
The AI proceeds to ask the core qualifying questions:
- "What is your current notice period?"
- "What is your expected salary range?"
- "Do you have experience with [Specific Tech/Skill]?"
- "Do you have the right to work in the UK?"

### 3. Immediate Outcome and Next Steps
If the candidate matches the criteria, the AI can provide more details about the role or even offer a link to the recruiter's calendar. If they aren't a fit for this specific role, the AI can offer to keep their details on file for future opportunities.
