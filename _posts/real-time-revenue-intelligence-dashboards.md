## 1. Omnichannel Activity Monitoring
Revenue intelligence doesn't just track "deals"; it tracks the *activity* that leads to deals. AI dashboards show you exactly how many conversations are happening on WhatsApp vs. Webchat, what the average sentiment is, and which channels are trending toward conversion. This allows RevOps to pivot resources to the highest-performing channels instantly.

## 2. Automated Pipeline Risk Detection
An AI-driven command center acts as an early warning system. By monitoring conversational intent and response velocities, the intelligence engine can flag "stalled" deals or declining lead quality across specific segments. This allows management to intervene while there is still time to save the revenue.

## 3. Prescriptive Growth Insights
Modern revenue intelligence doesn't just show you data; it tells you what to do. The AI analyzes patterns across your entire revenue lifecycle and surfaces specific recommendations: "WhatsApp response times for Real Estate leads are lagging—increase automation here," or "Expansion intent is high in the E-commerce segment—launch an upsell campaign."
