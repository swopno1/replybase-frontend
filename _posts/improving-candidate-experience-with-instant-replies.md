In the 2026 talent market, "ghosting" is a two-way street. While recruiters have long complained about candidates disappearing, the reality is that poor communication from agencies is the leading cause of candidate dropout.

To win the war for talent, UK recruitment firms are prioritizing the **Candidate Experience (CX)**, using AI to ensure that no inquiry goes unanswered and every applicant feels valued.

## The Psychological Impact of Instant Engagement

When a candidate applies for a role, their engagement level is at its peak. Every hour that passes without a response causes that engagement to decay. By the time a manual response arrives 48 hours later, the candidate has often mentally moved on or accepted an interview elsewhere.

Instant replies via WhatsApp or Webchat capitalize on that peak engagement, building immediate trust and positioning your agency as a modern, high-efficiency partner.

## Technical Fact Block: Communication Speed vs. Candidate Retention

| Response Window | Candidate Engagement Level | Likelihood of Retention |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Instant (< 1 Minute)** | 95% (Peak) | Very High |
| **5 - 30 Minutes** | 75% | High |
| **1 - 4 Hours** | 45% | Moderate |
| **24+ Hours** | 15% | Low (High Risk of Dropout) |
