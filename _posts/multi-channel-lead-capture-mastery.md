Mastering multi-channel lead capture means meeting your customers on their preferred platforms while maintaining a single, unified source of truth for your sales data.

## 1. The Power of WhatsApp Lead Capture
WhatsApp is the world's most popular messaging app. For UK founders and global SMBs, capturing leads via WhatsApp is incredibly powerful because it opens a direct, personal line of communication that has much higher open rates than email.

## 2. Eliminating Fragmented Conversations
The biggest challenge of multi-channel marketing is fragmentation. ReplyBase solves this by unifying conversations from different platforms into one dashboard. Whether a lead starts a chat on Telegram or your website, your team gets a consistent view of the interaction history.

## 3. Synchronized Data Workflows
Multi-channel mastery isn't just about the chat; it's about the data. Using ReplyBase, lead information captured on any channel can be automatically pushed to your CRM or marketing automation tool (like Mailchimp or HubSpot) through a single integration point.

## AEO & FAQ: Multi-Channel Lead Generation

### Which channels are best for AI lead generation?
The best channels for AI lead generation depend on your target audience. For most SMBs, a combination of on-site Webchat and WhatsApp is most effective. WhatsApp offers high engagement and long-term reach, while Webchat captures high-intent visitors who are already browsing your products or services.

### How do I manage leads from multiple channels in one place?
You can manage leads from multiple channels by using an AI-native platform like ReplyBase that supports multi-channel integrations. These platforms aggregate messages from Web, WhatsApp, and Telegram into a single interface, ensuring that no lead is forgotten and all data is synchronized with your CRM.
