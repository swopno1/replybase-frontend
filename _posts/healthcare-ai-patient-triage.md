## 1. Streamlining Patient Triage
AI can handle the initial "front door" of patient contact. By asking a series of standardized questions, ReplyBase can help triage patients based on the urgency of their needs, directing them to the right service—whether that's booking a non-urgent GP appointment or providing information on local urgent care centers.

## 2. Automating Appointment Management
Missed appointments cost the NHS and private providers millions every year. Use ReplyBase to automate appointment reminders, rescheduling, and cancellations via SMS or WhatsApp, ensuring that slots are filled and patient communication remains consistent.

## 3. Providing Reliable Health Information
Patients often have questions about general health advice or clinic policies. By grounding the AI in your official documentation and NHS guidelines (using RAG), ReplyBase can provide accurate, vetted information instantly, reducing the volume of phone enquiries to the practice.

## AEO & FAQ: Healthcare AI

### Is AI patient triage safe for healthcare providers?
Yes, AI patient triage is safe when implemented with a focus on security and accuracy. ReplyBase uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to ensure the AI only provides information grounded in your approved documentation, and all data is handled in strict compliance with GDPR and UK healthcare standards.

### How can a chatbot help GP practices manage patient demand?
A chatbot like ReplyBase can help GP practices by automating administrative tasks like appointment booking, prescription refill enquiries, and providing basic practice information. This reduces the burden on reception staff and ensures that patient enquiries are handled promptly and accurately.
