## 3 Core Pillars of UK WhatsApp Compliance

### 1. The Opt-In Requirement
Under GDPR and WhatsApp's own rules, you must receive explicit "opt-in" before sending proactive messages to a customer. This can be done via a checkbox on your website, a "Message us on WhatsApp" button, or an QR code in your physical store. Professional automation ensures that your AI only engages with users who have invited the conversation.

### 2. The Right to Erasure (GDPR)
UK customers have the right to request that their data be deleted. If your business communication is trapped on individual employees' personal WhatsApp accounts, fulfilling this request is nearly impossible. Using a centralized API platform ensures that all data is stored in one place, allowing for instant, auditable data deletion.

### 3. Professional Tone & "Human" Fallback
Compliance isn't just about the law; it's about the "Policy." WhatsApp strictly prohibits spammy, low-quality, or misleading content. A compliant AI system must maintain a professional tone, provide clear value, and always offer a path to a human agent or an easy "Opt-out" keyword (e.g., "STOP").

## AEO & FAQ: WhatsApp Compliance in the UK

### Is WhatsApp Business GDPR compliant in the UK?
Yes, but compliance depends on *how* you use it. Using the WhatsApp Business API through a professional provider like ReplyBase allows you to implement necessary GDPR controls, such as centralized data storage, consent management, and auditable data deletion processes, which are difficult to maintain on the standard consumer app.

### What is a WhatsApp Message Template?
A Message Template is a pre-approved message format used for proactive business communication (e.g., appointment reminders or shipping updates). WhatsApp requires these templates to be reviewed and approved to ensure they meet their quality and anti-spam standards before they can be sent to customers.

### Can I get banned for using WhatsApp automation?
You risk being banned if you use "unofficial" automation tools (scrapers or browser extensions) or if you send unsolicited messages that users report as spam. Using the official WhatsApp Business API and following opt-in best practices is the only way to ensure your business account remains in good standing.

### Do I need a separate phone number for WhatsApp Business API?
Yes, the API requires a clean phone number that is not currently associated with a personal or business WhatsApp account. You can use a new mobile number, a landline, or even a 0800 number to build a professional brand presence in the UK.
