# SaaS User Activation: Using WhatsApp AI to Reduce Churn and Boost Onboarding

For SaaS founders, the "Time to Value" (TTV) is the most important metric for survival. If a new user doesn't experience a "lightbulb moment" within the first hour—or even the first few minutes—of signing up, they are unlikely to ever become a paying customer.

In 2026, the most successful SaaS platforms are ditching long onboarding emails in favor of **WhatsApp-native activation.**

## The Problem: The "Empty State" Abandonment

Most SaaS churn happens in the first 24 hours. A user signs up, hits a "blank slate" dashboard, gets confused, and leaves. Traditional onboarding emails are often sent too late or filtered into the "Promotions" tab, where they are never seen.
