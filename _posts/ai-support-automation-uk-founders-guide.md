## Implementing AI Support: A 3-Step Workflow

Transitioning to automated support doesn't have to be complex. Here is the recommended workflow for UK businesses:

### 1. Audit Your Common Queries
Identify the 10-20 questions your support team answers most frequently (e.g., "Where is my order?", "How do I reset my password?"). These are the prime candidates for AI automation.

### 2. Connect Your Knowledge Base
Instead of manual flow building, modern AI platforms like ReplyBase can "read" your existing documentation, FAQs, and website content to generate accurate answers instantly.

### 3. Deploy Across Channels
Start with your most active channel—usually Webchat or WhatsApp—and monitor the AI's performance. As confidence grows, expand to other channels to create a seamless omnichannel experience.
