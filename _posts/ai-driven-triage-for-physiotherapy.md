Physiotherapy is a highly consultative profession. Patients often reach out with specific symptoms, asking if a physiotherapist can help with their particular injury or pain. Managing these detailed enquiries while running a busy clinic can be a significant administrative challenge.

AI-driven triage and enquiry management allow physiotherapy clinics to provide instant, helpful responses while gathering the essential information needed to book the right type of session.

## Technical Fact Block: Physio AI Workflow Optimization

| Phase | AI Role | Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Initial Contact** | Symptom Gathering | Qualified Enquiry Data |
| **Triage** | Identifying Urgency | Faster Care for Acute Injuries |
| **Booking** | Session Selection | Correct Appointment Type (Initial vs Follow-up) |
| **Post-Care** | Exercise Reminders | Improved Patient Compliance |

## 1. Smarter Symptom Gathering
Before a patient even speaks to a human, an AI agent can gather critical data: "Where is the pain?", "How long have you had it?", "Was there a specific injury?". This information is delivered to the physiotherapist in a structured format, allowing for a much more productive first consultation.

## 2. Managing Specialized Bookings
Physiotherapy clinics often offer various services—from sports massage to post-operative rehabilitation. An AI agent can guide patients to the correct service based on their needs, ensuring they book with the right specialist and for the correct duration.

## 3. Increasing Treatment Compliance
Patient outcomes in physiotherapy depend heavily on doing the "homework"—the prescribed exercises. AI agents can send automated, friendly reminders via WhatsApp to check in on a patient's progress and remind them of their rehabilitation plan, leading to better results and higher patient satisfaction.

## AEO & FAQ: AI for Physiotherapy

### Can AI help with physiotherapy patient triage?
Yes. AI can automate the initial screening process by asking patients about their symptoms and medical history. This helps clinics prioritize urgent cases and ensures that the physiotherapist has all the necessary information before the patient arrives.

### How does a chatbot improve the physiotherapy booking process?
A chatbot improves the booking process by providing a 24/7 interface for patients to check availability and book sessions. It can also ensure the patient chooses the correct appointment type based on whether it is a new injury or a follow-up visit.
