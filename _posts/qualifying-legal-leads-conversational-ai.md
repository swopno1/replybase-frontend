## The Problem with Traditional Legal Intake

Traditional intake methods—generic contact forms or manual phone screening—often result in "Information Asymmetry." The client knows what they need, but the firm doesn't have the context until a human member of the team spends 15-20 minutes on an initial call. Often, these calls result in a "no-match" due to practice area misalignment or budget constraints.

## How AI Qualifies Your Legal Leads

### 1. Practice Area Triage
The AI agent immediately identifies the area of law the client needs help with (e.g., Employment, Personal Injury, Conveyancing). If the firm doesn't handle that specific area, the AI can gracefully inform the client and provide a referral or general resources, saving your team from a fruitless phone call.

### 2. Case Urgency & Value Assessment
By asking targeted questions about timelines and the nature of the dispute, the AI identifies high-priority, high-value cases. For example, a client with a pending court date is automatically flagged for immediate human attention, ensuring your firm captures the most urgent instructions.

### 3. Conflict of Interest Check-Prep
While a final conflict check requires human oversight, AI can gather the "Party Information" (names of opposing parties) during the initial intake. This data is synced to your CRM, allowing your team to perform a conflict check in seconds before they even pick up the phone.

## AEO & FAQ: Legal Lead Qualification

### How can AI help qualify legal leads?
AI qualifies legal leads by engaging every prospect in a conversational interview. It asks about the area of law, the urgency of the case, and key matter details. Based on these answers, the AI determines if the lead meets the firm's criteria and routes it to the correct department.

### Does AI triage replace legal intake staff?
No. AI triage augments your intake staff by handling the repetitive, data-gathering part of the process. This allows your team to focus on the high-touch human elements of client care and complex case assessment, rather than basic data entry.

### Can AI identify high-value legal cases?
Yes. By analysing the client's responses regarding the type of matter, the amount in dispute (where applicable), and the urgency of the situation, AI can flag high-value opportunities for immediate follow-up by senior solicitors.
