## 3 Ways AI Automation Directly Boosts Profit

### 1. Drastic Reduction in Cost-per-Interaction
A human support interaction can cost between £3 and £10 when you factor in salary, benefits, and management overhead. A ReplyBase AI interaction costs pennies. By shifting 80% of your volume to AI, you instantly add thousands to your monthly net profit.

### 2. Capturing "Moment-of-Intent" Sales
If a customer is ready to buy but has one question about shipping, they won't wait for an email. They'll leave. AI resolves that question in seconds, capturing a sale that would have been lost to a competitor.

### 3. Scaling Without "Headcount Bloat"
The most expensive part of a growing business is hiring. AI allows you to grow your revenue by 2x, 5x, or 10x without doubling your support team. This "non-linear growth" is the key to achieving venture-scale margins in e-commerce.
