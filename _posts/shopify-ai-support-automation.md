## 1. Automating the "Where is my order?" (WISMO)
The most common enquiry for any e-commerce store is order tracking. ReplyBase connects directly to your Shopify backend, allowing customers to check their order status instantly via chat. This provides immediate satisfaction for the customer and massive time savings for you.

## 2. Personalizing the Shopping Experience
AI doesn't have to be cold and transactional. ReplyBase can use customer data from Shopify to provide personalized product recommendations based on past purchases or current browsing behavior, turning your chatbot into an automated personal shopper.

## 3. Handling Returns and Exchanges
Automate the first steps of the returns process. ReplyBase can guide customers through your return policy, collect the necessary details (order number, reason for return, photos), and even generate return labels via Zapier or your logistics provider.
