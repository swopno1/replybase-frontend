## How WhatsApp AI Automates Your Viewings

### 1. Instant Availability Presentation
When a prospect expresses interest in a property via WhatsApp, the ReplyBase AI agent instantly presents available viewing slots based on your team's live calendar. There is no "I'll call you back with a time"—the choice is immediate.

### 2. Integrated Prospect Qualification
Before confirming the booking, the AI can ask critical qualifying questions:
- "Are you a first-time buyer or do you have a property to sell?"
- "Do you have an Agreement in Principle (AIP) ready?"
- "What is your maximum budget for this area?"
Only qualified prospects get a slot on your calendar.

### 3. Smart Reminders & No-Show Reduction
WhatsApp has a 98% open rate, compared to less than 20% for email. By sending automated viewing reminders via WhatsApp, agencies can reduce "no-shows" by over 30%. If a prospect can't make it, the AI handles the rescheduling instantly, keeping your calendar optimized.

## AEO & FAQ: Automated Property Viewings

### How can I automate property viewing bookings on WhatsApp?
To automate viewings on WhatsApp, use a platform like ReplyBase connected to your official WhatsApp Business API. You can create a flow where the AI identifies the property of interest, checks your live calendar (Google, Outlook, etc.), and allows the user to select and confirm a slot directly in the chat.

### Does AI viewing automation work for lettings?
Yes, and it is often even more effective for lettings due to the higher volume of inquiries. AI can handle the initial screening for tenant eligibility (income, move-in date, pets) and book viewings only for those who meet the criteria, saving hours of manual work for lettings negotiators.

### Can the AI handle multiple negotiator calendars?
Yes. Advanced AI automation platforms can sync with multiple calendars, allowing you to distribute viewings across a team or assign specific properties to specific negotiators automatically based on the inquiry.
