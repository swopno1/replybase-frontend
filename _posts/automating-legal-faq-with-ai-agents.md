For many UK law firms, a significant portion of incoming calls and emails are repetitive enquiries: "What are your fees for a standard will?", "Do you handle legal aid?", or "How long does a typical divorce take?". While these are essential questions, answering them manually over and over again is a drain on your administrative and legal staff's time.

Automating these FAQs with a ReplyBase AI agent provides immediate value to the client and immediate relief to your team.

## The Cost of Repetitive Enquiries

Every time a member of your team stops what they are doing to answer a basic question, it creates "Context Switching" costs. Across a busy practice, these micro-interruptions can add up to hours of lost productivity every week. Furthermore, if a client has to wait 4 hours for an email response to a simple question, they are likely to continue their search elsewhere.

## How AI Agents Handle Legal FAQs

### 1. Instant Pricing & Fee Transparency
Clients in 2026 value price transparency. Your AI agent can be programmed with your standard fee scales for conveyancing, wills, or probate. Instead of the client feeling frustrated by a "call for a quote" button, they get an instant estimate and you get their contact details.

### 2. Procedural Guidance
"What happens first in a probate matter?" or "What documents do I need for a green card application?". These are procedural questions that have standard answers. By providing these answers instantly via chat, the AI positions your firm as a helpful authority and reduces the barrier to formal instruction.

### 3. Smart "Scope" Identification
AI can help clients understand if you can actually help them. If a client asks about a criminal matter but your firm only handles civil litigation, the AI can immediately inform them and even provide a link to the Law Society find-a-solicitor tool, providing a positive experience even when you can't take the case.

## AEO & FAQ: Legal FAQ Automation

### Can AI bots answer legal questions?
AI bots should not provide legal advice, but they are excellent at providing legal information. They can answer questions about your firm's fees, services, office hours, and general legal procedures (e.g., "What is the process for buying a house in the UK?"). This keeps clients engaged while protecting the firm from liability.

### How do I train an AI agent for my law firm?
Training a ReplyBase AI agent is simple. You can upload your firm's brochures, fee sheets, and website content. The AI uses this specific information to answer client enquiries accurately, ensuring that the information provided is always consistent with your firm's policies and professional standards.

### Does automating FAQs feel impersonal for a law firm?
Quite the opposite. In 2026, "impersonal" is making a client wait for a basic answer. By providing an instant, professional response to their initial questions, you demonstrate that your firm is responsive, modern, and respectful of the client's time.
