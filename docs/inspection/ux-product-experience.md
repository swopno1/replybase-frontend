# UX Product Experience - Inspection Report

## Landing Flow
1. **Hook:** Hero headline + Visual.
2. **Validation:** Features + Pricing.
3. **Action:** CTA to Trial.

## Friction Points
- The documentation link in the footer leads to a detailed technical area. Ensure a "Getting Started" guide is the first thing a new user sees.
- The pricing table "supported channels" list is long. Use icons for faster visual processing.

## Onboarding (Marketing Side)
- The "Concierge onboarding" for Business is a major differentiator. Should be highlighted more prominently in the pricing header, not just the footer of the table.
