## Ongoing Content Standards (every piece, every time)

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Minimum length | 2,000 words for supporting posts, 3,000+ for pillars |
| Keyword research | Must verify search volume in Google Keyword Planner before writing |
| AEO | Minimum 5 FAQ pairs with `###` heading structure per post |
| GEO | Minimum 2 cited statistics from external sources (Ofcom, ONS, Statista) |
| Author | Always "Shaun, ReplyBase Founder" — never "ReplyBase Team" |
| Internal links | Minimum 3 internal links per post |
| CTA | Always link to trial signup with UTM source: `?source=blog_[slug]` |
| Image | Featured image required — even a simple branded graphic |

## What NOT to do

- Do not publish a new post without completing keyword research first
- Do not use "ReplyBase Team" as the author — E-E-A-T requires a real person
- Do not write another generic "AI for [industry]" post without a unique angle
- Do not claim "hundreds of customers" or similar unverifiable social proof
- Do not target keywords with >50 keyword difficulty until Tier 1 keywords are ranking
