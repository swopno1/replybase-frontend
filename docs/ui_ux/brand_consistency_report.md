# Brand Consistency Report

## Visual Assets
- **Logo:** Consistent across navbar and footer.
- **Iconography:** Uses Lucide-react. Consistent style.
- **Imagery:** Minimal use of images currently.

## Voice & Tone
- **Tone:** Professional, reliable, forward-looking.
- **Consistency:** High. The messaging across About, Privacy, and Landing pages remains consistent.

## Brand Gaps
- **Identity:** Needs a "signature" visual element (e.g., a specific pattern, glow, or character) that makes ReplyBase instantly recognizable.
- **Color Palette:** Too reliant on "default" indigo. Consider a custom brand blue or purple.
