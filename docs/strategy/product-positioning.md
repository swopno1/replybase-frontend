# Product Positioning Strategy

## Target Audience
- **Primary:** Tech-forward SMBs (Small-Medium Businesses) looking to scale customer support without linear headcount growth.
- **Secondary:** Agencies managing multiple client accounts.

## Value Proposition
"ReplyBase provides the most accessible yet powerful AI-native conversation platform for businesses that value both innovation and reliability."

## Key Differentiators
1. **Multi-Channel Native:** Built for WhatsApp, Telegram, and Webchat from day one.
2. **Technical Excellence:** High-performance AI responses with low latency.
3. **Transparent Pricing:** No hidden fees, clear overage costs, 14-day free trial.

## Perception Goal
From "A chatbot tool" to "Our AI Customer Success Partner."
